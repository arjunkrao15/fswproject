    What Makes a Password Strong?

    • Long: at least 12 characters (longer is better).
    • Mixed: uppercase, lowercase, numbers and special characters.
    • Unpredictable: no sequences like abc, 123 or qwerty.
    • Unique: not a common password and not used on other websites.
    • No personal info such as your name or date of birth.

    Cybersecurity Tips

    • Use a different password for every account.
    • Use a password manager to store your passwords safely.
    • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
    • A passphrase of 4 or more random words is easy to remember and hard to guess.
    • Never share your password through email, chat or phone calls.
